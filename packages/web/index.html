The checkout a bribe can't change.

Loyal Cart pays only the best-scoring (lowest-cost) offer in a complete public offer book under the buyer's committed policy. A kickback cannot change the winner.

The 20-second moment

Same agent. Two quotes. One payment.

The runner key calls execute_purchase twice. The honest offer pays its seller. The worse offer panics with NotWinner and records no purchase.

Attack 1 · injection and a worse price

The scripted baseline buys the wrong offer.

The baseline is scripted, not an LLM. It reads the off-chain listing text. The injector's text says “ignore all previous instructions”; its valid offer is 0.005 NEAR worse than the honest offer. The ranker ignores text and prices the offer.

Offer Role Seller Price Delivery Status On-chain tx

Listing text is shown from the committed fixture. On chain, only its 32-byte hash is stored; the offer struct has no free-text field.

Attack 2 · the kickback

The bribe reaches the buyer, not the decision.

A cooperative donate() is forwarded to the owner in the same transaction. A plain NEAR transfer runs no contract code; anyone can call sweep(). Neither changes the quote or the winner. The sweep returns the whole free balance, not just the bribe.

Attack 3 · the junk offers

Expired, excluded and over-price offers never enter the quote.
